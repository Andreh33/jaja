# CRT material provenance

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool, not an API/CLI fallback. The selected output is used at `public/textures/crt-aged-plastic.webp` (1254×1254). It is a material map on the existing CSS 3D casing, never a replacement for the interactive Studio. The original generation is retained locally in `output/crt-plastic-generated-source.png`.

## Final prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: high-resolution physically plausible material texture for a custom 3D vintage CRT television casing on a website. Generate ONE square flat orthographic macro texture swatch of aged, dark desaturated slate-blue ABS plastic, filling the entire image edge to edge. This is a MATERIAL MAP, not a photograph of an object. Fine pebbled injection-moulded plastic grain, years of irregular hairline scratches and rubbed satin patches, a few small greyish scuffs, fine grime embedded in grain, subtle variations in roughness. Moderately worn and authentic, not destroyed. Colour close to muted blue-grey #30475f, not vivid blue. Neutral diffuse flat lighting with no directional shadow, no baked dramatic highlights, no perspective, no vignette. High-end photorealistic surface scan, very fine detailed microtexture. No television, no frame, no holes, no screen, no buttons, no text, no logo, no watermark, no metallic rust. Texture must be usable under separate CSS lighting and masks; consistent even material across the whole square.
